# Placement-first strategy

Next action: make every decision maximize survival, not a local damage race.

1. **Objective.** Placement is the death order: first death is 16th, last alive is 1st; simultaneous deaths tie, and survivors at 8:00 rank by HP ([protocol §4](../protocol.md)). This makes avoiding a fight generally worth more than converting a low-probability kill. The simulator in `eval/sim.py` is explicitly only an approximation, so it must not be used as evidence of real-match win rate.
2. **Opening and midgame.** Move quietly through walkable cover toward the next zone, scan with aim, and disengage rather than trade when another mech is near. Boost only to escape, cross exposed ground, or beat zone damage: boosting creates audible sound, while slow walking/standing is silent (protocol §§7–9). Heal by remaining out of combat for `regen_delay`; zone damage prevents regen (protocol §7).
3. **Combat.** Face the likely shooter while retreating: rear hits use `rear_mult`, default ×1.5 (protocol §7). Keep the neutral gun for safe ranged finishing. Prefer laser against mortar, mortar against lightning, and lightning against laser only when range/cover makes the stated weapon triangle favorable (protocol §7); do not pursue loot through a shrinking zone.
4. **Cubes and drops.** Cubes improve capped HP/damage but enlarge the mech without a size cap, making it easier to hit (protocol §10). Take nearby safe cubes early; after the damage/HP cap, treat extra cubes mostly as liability unless they unlock a valuable upgrade. Supply drops grant three cubes, full HP and energy but need four seconds alone in the ring (defaults; read live config), so approach late, with cover and an exit rather than contesting a populated ring (protocol §7).
5. **Ranked improvements.** (1) Maintain a threat score from visible enemies, sounds, beams, and recent damage; retreat/face threats first (§§7–8). (2) Use obstacle-aware routes and reachable safe points inside the *current* zone before its shrink (§§7, 9). (3) Add gun lead plus line-of-sight/fire discipline (§7). (4) Select upgrades using live offer effects: armor, mobility, regeneration and sensors support survival (§10). (5) Contest drops only when the predicted ring is empty and zone-safe (§7).

Failure modes: blindly chasing cubes or drops creates predictable fights; staying outside a shrinking zone erases HP and disables regen; holding a laser telegraphs aim; mortar self-splash hurts; and queueing old observations produces dropped actions (protocol §§2, 7–10).
